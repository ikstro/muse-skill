# Lanework — product sheet (fictional)

Lanework is a fictional product invented for this example. Its name, facts, prices and customers do not describe any real company.

## What it is
A task tracker for teams of 3 to 15 people, in the browser.

## Product truths
- T1 · Every task has exactly one owner and one due date. There are no unassigned tasks; a task without an owner cannot be saved.
- T2 · The Friday summary writes itself from what moved this week: shipped, moved, late, with owners. One click sends it to the team or a client.
- T3 · Quiet hours: notifications reach each person only inside their own working hours; anything after hours waits until morning.
- T4 · Paste a spreadsheet and get a board: owners, dates and status are read from the columns in one paste.
- T5 · $6 per person per month. Free for teams of up to 3.
- T6 · The Monday view: one screen that shows who owns what this week, readable in about 30 seconds.
