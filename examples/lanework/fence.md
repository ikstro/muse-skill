# FENCE — Lanework landing page: hero + first screen
Mode: persuade (the visitor decides to try it; the action is "Start free")
Target: the first viewport of the Lanework marketing site. Lanework is a fictional product (see `product.md`).
Confirmation: the fence content was approved as part of the run plan (owner, 2026-10-22).

LOCKS
- Lanework design system: Figtree only; type scale, colours, buttons, nav, panels and task rows as in `refs/lanework-ds.md` · decided (owner) 2026-10-22 · implemented in `comps/lanework-ds.css`
- Backgrounds: white or surface #f4f4f1 · decided (owner)
- Primary action "Start free" (accent button); the price line "$6 per person / month · free for 3" near it · decided (owner)
- Copy in English · decided (owner)
- Product facts only from `product.md` (T1–T6); no invented customers, logos or statistics · decided (owner)
- Product UI is shown as real, working HTML of the product, never as a screenshot in a browser frame · decided (owner)

PRINCIPLES
- One product as the protagonist: the list of who owns what.
- One promise per viewport.
- Calm, not busy: one thing moves at a time.
- Show the task, not the dashboard.
- Every name, date and number on the page comes from the same mock world.

AUDIENCE
- Who: the lead of a small studio or product team, 6 people, who runs the work and also does it.
- Moment of the problem: Monday, 9:40. The standup is in its 25th minute and nobody can say who owns the client's invoice fix.
- Uses today: a shared spreadsheet that nobody updates, plus a chat thread as the to-do list.
- Would show a friend: the Friday summary that wrote itself and went straight to the client.
- Stops them buying: "another tool everyone has to keep updated"; "migrating our sheet will take a week".

PRODUCT TRUTHS (from product.md, fictional)
- T1 Every task has exactly one owner and one due date; no unassigned tasks.
- T2 The Friday summary writes itself from what moved; one click sends it.
- T3 Quiet hours: notifications only inside each person's working hours.
- T4 Paste a spreadsheet, get a board, in one paste.
- T5 $6 per person per month; free for up to 3.
- T6 The Monday view: who owns what this week, readable in about 30 seconds.

FREE AXES
- Promise / H1: open.
- Product presentation: which part of the product, at what scale, in what state, with whose data.
- Moment: what happens in the first three seconds and what the visitor can do.
- Composition and rhythm: where the product sits, how much quiet.
- Proof: which truth is demonstrated rather than stated.
- Authored content: the tasks, names, dates in the mock world.
- Typographic delivery: placement and measure inside the type scale.
- World / metaphor: open, last.

CLICHÉS (excluded)
- A product screenshot inside a browser frame, floating and tilted over a gradient blob.
- "All your work in one place", "Work smarter, not harder", "The future of work".
- Three feature cards with an icon, a heading and two lines.
- A logo wall "Trusted by teams at…".
- Predictable opposite: a black terminal page, monospace everything, "ship faster" bravado.

SELLING MECHANICS (kept, authored)
- The real product, big and early.
- One promise.
- The price next to the action.
- The product doing its one job in front of you.

TASTE (soft, from taste.md)
- The product in action, in motion; real UI beats a drawn re-creation.
- One protagonist, singled out; numbers agree across the page.

OWNER'S PICTURE: none. BAR: none.
