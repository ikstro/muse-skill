## What the replay shows

1. **Persuade did not regress.** Lanework reproduced the featured four; totals moved by at most two points (23, 23, 20, 19 against 22, 21, 20, 20). On the Duo list three of four held and the fourth place moved by one point: the fourth slot is noise of about two points between two juries of the same list. A pick that lands fifth is not a rubric error; a pick that lands fifteenth is.
2. **Operate, two causes of the two misses.**
   - Koto: the owner picked on look («точно такой же LEGO Horizon стиль»): controls made of the world's own material. The 1.2 rubric scored the look 1 on OWN by design (only behaviour counted), so the pick fell to 15. The rule was wrong for products whose world is what they sell (a game, a toy). Fixed in operate.md §3: when the fence lists the product's own material as a free axis, a look made of that material scores on OWN as a behaviour would; a borrowed look still 0. The rejected «Як у справжній грі» (a look from another game) stays at 0 under the new rule.
   - Altype: the owner's pick «ghbdtn → привет → hello → 🔊» scored 14, fifteenth. The jury found no Operate type for it ("a demo loop"), gave DONE 2 and a penalty. The owner used it as the window's own home state, a demonstration before any action; in 1.2 that type ("the first three seconds") exists only on a hybrid, so on a plain Operate surface the entry had no home in the rubric. Not fixed by rule; see "Not resolved". The rejected «Select. ⇧⇧. Done.» featured at 18: its list entry names no bottom sheet (the sheet came in the build), so FIT had nothing to catch at the list stage, while «The keyboard map», which assigns in a sheet, did fail RULES. Fixed in operate.md §3 so a build cannot add one either: a control pattern from another platform fails FIT, with the bottom sheet named. Five entries tied at 18 for two slots and three of them left the run unrecorded: the tie rule now breaks by dice and sends the loser at 18+ to record.md, so the next run of the same project sees it first.
3. **Hybrid (WhiteCore) behaves as planned.** The three widget-level behaviours the owner made signature and always-on rose; the four the owner dropped are in the bottom eight; «Search opens the route» moved from 14 to 17 and into the featured four. The entry the persuade jury had crowned at 23 («The answer to their question») fell to 17 because the figure "from 517.15" hides that the fare has no bag: a TRUST finding the owner's own remark («непонятно, какое это отличие») supports.
4. **Identity holds.** The owner's pick was featured; the combined source lost the fourth place on a die at 15. The replay named what the Krya run's jury also named: seven round-head variants, no state in the tray. Those became the silhouette cap and the SYSTEM places in identity.md.
5. **Figure checks earn their place.** The WhiteCore replay found a multiplication the engine never did (129.86 × 2 ≠ 259.73), a "0.00" total before any choice, and a "from" price that hides its fare. These are the Operate tells on a working surface; the jury catches them before the build.

## Changes made from the replay

- operate.md §3: OWN counts a look made of the product's own material when the fence frees that axis; FIT fails a control pattern from another platform; ties for the last place break by dice and the loser at 18+ goes to record.md.
- identity.md: at most three entries per silhouette; entry fields "how the mark fills the 16 px square", "Lockup", "Joins"; a seed constraint table for marks; SYSTEM names the places a mark lives (menu bar template icon, favicon on a dark tab, Windows tray); a LOCKS gate; a re-roll rule on READ; provenance tags never in the fence.
- ideation.md board: featured entries keep their strips, the frame is for the rest; combine parts are the entry's own fields; the (combined) card gets a strip from the board agent and "combined from" until the jury scores it.
- finish.md: the WhiteCore owner combined seven parts, and "every part in the four" can never be met on an operate surface where the owner builds a prototype of switches. On Operate the calibration line now reads hit when every entry the owner marked always-on or signature was in the featured four.
