## Featured

Top by total, no two of the same type (as written). E07 (17, transformation) is second overall but shares its type with E22 and drops out under the rule; the 15-point tie between E20, E21 and E25 was broken by a die (E25).

- **E22 · 19 · transformation / the letterform**: the only entry that reads as a bold, serious mark at 16, belongs to this name in both alphabets, and carries the duck in one cut instead of a drawing.
- **E13 · 16 · product as object / the glyph**: puts the duck into the keyboard's own symbol vocabulary; the keyboard-is-the-interface principle as a mark. Weak at 16 on its own; strong in the "⌘K"-style lockup.
- **E26 · 16 · the wordmark alone / the wordmark + the glyph**: the board's own heading; typed rather than drawn, the product's own faces and characters, the developer joke told straight. Best from a metre; the 16 px (')> needs pixel placement to keep the beak.
- **E25 · 15 · identity/social / the pair**: krya / кря on a shared k says who it is for; held back by the plate it needs at 16 and by overlap with E22's shared-K idea.

Worth drawing for the owner as a combination: E22's K-with-beak or E26's typed duck as the symbol, with E07's Z/Я logic as the tagline or launch moment ("rhz → кря").

## What is missing

- **Over-represented**: the round duck head plus one code glyph (E06, E08, E09, E11, E23, and near it E01, E16): seven variations of the fence's DuckDuckGo cliché with a different eye or beak. Also "a duck made of X" where X is a product feature drawn small (E04, E17, E24, E12): each loses the duck or the feature at 16.
- **The state is under-used**: no entry makes the tray mark show the current layout (EN / RU / UA) or "fixing now", although the tray is where the layout lives and the audience already reads the РУС/ENG indicator there. A mark that is also the layout indicator would replace something on screen, not add an icon. E07, E16, E19 touch it; none commits.
- **Nothing answers "a keyboard hook is a keylogger" at 16 px**: the offline truth appears only through captions (E08 "Local", E10 "pond", E11 "0 servers"), and every one of those marks needs the caption. No entry has a visual for "stays on this machine" that reads without words.
- **The tray row was not considered**: several marks collide with system icons (E19 update arrow, E24 volume, E08 refresh, E07/E25 the layout indicator). The board should show every mark inside a real Windows tray, light and dark, before anyone picks.
- **The accent is unspoken**: only E26 names a colour (duck yellow on dark). Yellow at 16 px on a light taskbar has weak contrast; no entry says what the mark does on the light taskbar.
- **The audience's language as a picture**: only E22, E07 and E25 use the Cyrillic/Latin overlap, the one material no rival outside this audience can own. A single wordmark in which "кря" and "krya" are one setting (not stacked) is not on the list.
- **Only a rival could not stage**: E22, E07, E26, E25 (name + both alphabets + the product's faces). Everything else survives a rename to any duck-named tool.
