## What is missing

- Over-represented: T1 (ownership). Ten entries prove "every task has an owner" (A, C, D, E, F, H, M, O, P, S); four of them are the same single-row reveal.
- Fear with no proof: "another tool everyone has to keep updated". G asserts the plan stays current, R implies it; nobody shows why updating Lanework is less work than the sheet (for example, the Friday summary being the only reason anyone needs to touch it).
- T5 (price) has only I, which fails. No passing entry places "$6 · free for 3" inside the scene; it is left to the lock beside the button.
- T6's "30 seconds" is claimed (B, C, J) but never measured in front of the visitor: no entry lets them read the Monday view against a real 30-second clock.
- T3 (quiet hours) has only L, and L is drawn (clocks, a moon); no entry shows quiet hours in the product's own UI.
- T4 (paste) has only T; the "week of migration" fear is answered once, so if T is not featured the board has no answer to it.
- Missing moment: the week as one arc. C (Monday 9:40) and N (Friday 16:55) are the bookends of the same week and could be one hero with the same tasks and names.
- Only Lanework could stage: C, N, H, P (the drop zone that refuses an unowned task), R (drag → the summary line rewrites). A rival could run E, F, O, K, Q and the generic halves of G and S by swapping the logo.
