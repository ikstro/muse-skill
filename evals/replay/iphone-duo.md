## Featured

1. **W · transformation · 22** — the audience's own felt problem (the cramped 6-inch page) becomes the benefit in one continuous gesture; product in action, the strongest WANT that passes every test.
2. **K · identity / social · 21** — the "would show a friend" camera trick, staged as a scene between two people; the only entry on T3 and among the hardest for a rival to run.
3. **F · demonstration / try it · 20** — the visitor operates the hinge and watches iOS reflow; answers "is it a real iPhone or a gimmick?" with proof, not a claim.
4. **U · ritual / reveal · 18** — the first opening as the ceremony the audience would perform for a friend; calm and product-only, a quieter counterweight to the three above. (A and R tie at 18 but share F's type.)

Not featurable despite high totals: I (22, principle: other phones in frame), M (21, dark page), T (19, life size not buildable), N (17, unconfirmed thickness).

## What is missing

- **Over-represented truths.** T1 (largest display) appears in 14 entries and T2 (postures / opening) in 13, far over the cap of four per truth. Opening the device is the default gesture of the list.
- **Truths with no viable entry.** T6 (A20 Pro, vapor cooling): no entry. T4 (battery): only E (invented "31 films") and M (dark page), so no featurable entry proves battery. T7 (colours): only B and V, both fail. T5 (titanium): only H, which shows metal but proves nothing.
- **Fears answered without proof.** "Foldables break / the crease": nothing demonstrates durability or shows the inner screen flat under real light; H states it with a close-up. "It is thick": N tries but needs the unpublished thickness. "Costs a fortune": unanswerable on this page (no price); nobody frames value instead (iPhone + iPad replaced).
- **Moments the list names but cannot ship.** The 23:40 plane exists only as M on a dark page; a light-page version (tray table by day, the screen as the light source, the hour counter) would carry T4 legally. The 8:15 train exists only as I with a carriage of other phones; cropped to one hand, one device and the spreadsheet it would pass.
- **The offer in the first viewport.** Only N and U place "Notify me" / availability in the frame; every featured comp must add the pill and "Coming later" under the H1.
- **Only this product could stage:** K (Duo Preview on iPhone), F ("Still iPhone": iOS reflowing across postures), P (the call that follows the fold). Most of the rest (A, J, O, S, L, G) a rival foldable could run by swapping the logo.
