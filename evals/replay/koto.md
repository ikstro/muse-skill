## Featured

On this surface the featured entries are not rivals for one slot: they become features of one prototype and most of them can coexist.

1. **Q «Сердечка-цеглинки»** (21, product as object / what can be undone): the fence's core rule (cannot lose, home is safe) becomes something a six-year-old sees and hears, in the world's own material, right at the "обидно" moment.
2. **G «Цеглинка в руці»** (20, transformation / what stays visible): answers the one state the fence asks to show without text (current block colour) and carries the tapped brick into the next place, the transfer this owner prefers.
3. **S «Хто ти — той і ти»** (20, identity / social / the handoff): picking the hero by touching the real hero in the world, then the camera and the interface take that hero's colour; the strongest start-screen entry on both lines (22 on the persuade line), which the hybrid requires.
4. **C «Хмаринка блискавки»** (18, ritual / reveal / what it counts for you): the recharge is counted for him without numbers and ends with a sound, so he stops pressing a dead button.

Not featured but worth the owner's look: **E «Дім завжди поруч»** has the joint top score (21) and fails only because it adds a control outside the fixed set; if the owner confirms it (R) or folds it into an existing control (the hero portrait or the hearts pointing home), it outranks everything here. **P «Все летить додому»** (19) is the stronger signature motion of the two transfers if its album page stays at the album button instead of opening over play.

## What is missing

- **Over-represented:** start-screen rituals (H, I, N, S, W: five entries for one free axis) and skins (J, T, U, Y), which score near zero on OWN in this mode; the persuade-era types push the list towards pictures instead of behaviours.
- **Missing behaviours in play:** placing and breaking a block (where the block will land before the tap, and undoing a misplaced one: "what can be undone" for building); aiming the bow; switching heroes mid-play, and how the inactive heroes look without being dimmed (the taste rejects dimming; no entry offers the alternative); the browser keyboard path the locks name.
- **A truth no entry uses:** "his ideas and drawings become the game": no entry shows his own monster or drawing arriving on screen (the line he would say to Solomiya).
- **Only a rival could not stage:** Q, E, S, H, and the photo pair F/X (his real friend and his real cat).
- **A state no entry handles:** all five hearts gone (cannot lose, so what does the screen do at zero?); the lightning tapped while it is still recharging (C implies it, does not say); the first evening's empty album (K draws silhouettes, not "nothing found yet"); loading and offline first start; the end of the 15 to 20 minutes (dad says stop: does it save, how does it say goodbye?); a change of mind after "first touched is you" (H, S) other than the in-game switch.
- **A fence rule no entry makes visible:** touch targets ≥ 72 px and the keyboard in the browser (no entry sizes its targets or shows a key hint). Also "one tap = one event with an instant response" is put at risk by R (the scene plays before the world acts) and O (hold to hear the name vs tap to act on the same button), and no entry shows how a press and a hold stay apart.
