# Identity: a mark, a logo, an icon

Steps 1 to 5 when the target is a mark: a logo, a wordmark, an app icon, a favicon, a tray icon. One run on record (a developer tool, 2026-10-30): 26 drawn marks on the board; the owner took the board's own heading, set in the product's faces, and one drawn glyph; the jury's "what is missing" was the most useful output; motion comps were not needed; the drawn marks that lost were a round head plus one code glyph, in seven variations. Two lessons: the type the board is set in is a candidate, and a mark has no three-beat moment unless it animates.

## 1. Idea types

At least five types in the list, at most five per type, and at most three entries that share one silhouette (a dot and a bar, a round head and a glyph) whatever their types: the record has one list where seven of twenty-six marks were a round head plus a code glyph, and another where a dot plus a bar appeared in eight of twenty entries across four types. The category's default mascot shape is the incumbent, entry zero; when the design system already carries a placeholder mark, it is a second incumbent, in the shuffle unmarked like the first.

- **the letterform**: the name set so that one letter or one pair carries the idea (a letter shared by two alphabets; a ligature that is the product's gesture).
- **the glyph**: a sign made of the product's own material (characters from its code, a key, a caret, a cursor).
- **the state**: the mark changes with what the product is doing (recording, paused, the current layout), so the mark is also the status.
- **the pun that passes in three seconds**: the name's meaning seen, not explained; allowed only when the picture is read before the name is.
- **the object**: the thing itself at icon size, drawn once, no plate.
- **the negative**: what is left out carries the idea (the space inside a letter is the symbol).
- **the pair**: two marks that only make sense together (light and dark, two languages, on and off).
- **the wordmark alone**: no symbol; the setting is the idea.

## 2. Entry shape

```
N. [type] «Name» — Mark: what you see at 128 px — At 16 px: what survives, and how the mark fills the square (full bleed, a glyph with air, a plate) — Lockup: how the mark sits with the name, or "alone" — Says: what it tells about the product, in the audience's words — Own: why only this product could carry it, the reason without a rating — Asset: T set in type · S drawn SVG · G needs a glyph or asset from the product — Joins: audience line + truth
```

The board's own heading, set in the project's faces, is always an entry: line one when the owner pictured it, otherwise a listed entry tagged `(board)`. Provenance tags (`(seed)`, `(board)`, `(owner)`, `(taste)`, `(record)`) live in the wide list only; the fence never names them, or the jury goes looking for the tagged entry.

Seeds as in ideation.md, about a quarter of the list, with the constraint table replaced by one for marks: one colour only · no curves · the name is the mark · fits a circle · the negative space is the sign · reads upside down · moves (under two seconds). The idea type, the audience line and the truth are drawn as in ideation.md.

## 3. Jury

Procedure as in [ideation.md](ideation.md): a fresh opus agent, the shuffled list, the fence, the anchors from `record.md` (same mode), two readings, "what is missing". The rubric:

1. As the person from the AUDIENCE block, meeting the mark:
   - **READ** 1–5, doubled: recognised in one second at 16 px in a row of other icons, and from a metre on a screen. The jury writes what they saw.
   - **SAY** 1–5: what the mark tells about the product, in their words. A mood scores 1; a specific thing the product does scores 5.
   - **LIKE** 1–5: do they want it on their screen, in their tray, on their bag.
2. As a creative director:
   - **OWN** 1–5: could a rival use it by changing the name? 5 means impossible.
   - **PENALTY** 0–2: the category's mascot default; the name pun as the only idea; a mark that needs a plate, a frame or a caption to work.
   - **SYSTEM** yes/no: works in one colour, reversed, in mono, at 16, 32 and 128 px, light and dark; survives the places it will live (a one-colour template icon in a macOS menu bar, an ink favicon on a dark tab, a Windows tray beside the system's own icons); if it animates, the animation is under two seconds and the still frame still reads.
   - **LOCKS** yes/no: keeps every lock in the fence (a locked letterform, a locked colour, a locked name). The dry run on record had a mark pass SYSTEM while breaking the locked capital of the name.

Total = 2×READ + SAY + LIKE + OWN − PENALTY (maximum 25). An entry that fails SYSTEM or LOCKS is listed and cannot be featured. Re-roll: if no entry beats the incumbent on READ, the list is re-rolled before the sheets are drawn, and the owner is told. Ties break by dice; a loser at 18 or more goes to `record.md` as unpicked.

## 4. Show: sheets, not comps

Every entry that passed SYSTEM is drawn (type or SVG, on the project's faces and colours, no stock icon), and shown on sheets, not as cards with moments:

- one sheet per size: 128, 32 and 16 px, every mark in the same order, light and dark;
- one sheet of every mark at 16 px inside a real row: a browser tab strip, a system tray, a phone home screen, so the owner reads it where it will live;
- the states, where the entry has them (recording, paused, the layout), as a strip;
- a moment only for a mark that animates: two seconds at most, recorded as a strip.

Conventions: a pair shows both halves side by side at 128 and 32 and its favicon half alone at 16; a state entry shows its default still on the size sheets and every state on the strip; a letterform shows its lockup under the 128 px mark; the incumbents are drawn when they passed the gates; featured cells are outlined in the project's one accent. The sheets are drawn by a sonnet agent that did not write the list.

The owner picks, combines (the type of one with the glyph of another is common) or promotes; combinations are drawn on the next sheet.

## 5. Critic and finish

The critic receives the sheets, the fence, the Identity tells from [finish.md](finish.md) and the quality references; it judges at 16 px first, then at 128. The restraint pass asks of every stroke whether the mark still reads without it. Finish as in finish.md; the record line names which type won and whether it came from the board's own type.
