# Record

What the owner did with the ideas Muse showed them: every featured, promoted and picked entry of every run, every entry that scored 18 or more without being featured, and every dropped entry, with the jury's score and the owner's verdict in their own words. This is how Muse learns. It is read at the fence step twice: entries from the same project may come back into the list, tagged `(record)` until the jury; entries from the same mode become the jury's anchors, up to six, three picked and three rejected, so the jury calibrates on this owner rather than on itself. It is appended at the finish step.

Start with this file empty below the headings. The lines below come from the Lanework example run (in `examples/lanework/`) and from two invented projects that show the format; delete them before your first run.

Format: `- YYYY-MM-DD · <project> · <mode> · [type] «hook» · jury n/25 · verdict: picked | combined into «…» | rejected | unpicked · owner: «their words» · could live: <where the idea could still be used>`

Verdicts: **picked** means built as the direction; **combined** means a part of it (its hook, moment, proof or gesture) went into the chosen direction; **rejected** means the owner said no, and the words say why; **unpicked** means featured or promoted and left without a reaction.
